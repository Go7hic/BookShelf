## Design Context

### Users

Developers and creative teams use `BookShelf` to present a curated collection as a reusable React component. The immediate job is to browse a seven-volume editorial shelf, select a book, and inspect or open it without losing the physical continuity of the shelf.

### Brand Personality

Editorial, tactile, and quietly cinematic. The experience should feel like a carefully lit design-library installation: composed rather than decorative, physical rather than app-like, and confident without visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction

The accepted reference is a continuous horizontal shelf with seven books, a dominant central volume, intentional clipping at both edges, a restrained dark room, walnut shelving, and soft studio light. Covers may use original artwork and copy, but must read as cloth-bound hardcovers with paper, foil, depth, and grain. Multi-level navigation is an extension of this scene and must not change the single-level composition.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve the reference camera composition before adding features.
2. Make material response visible: cloth grain, page edges, foil, soft contact shadows, and rounded boards.
3. Use motion to communicate a physical book state, with one coherent shelf-to-detail choreography.
4. Keep the interaction shell quiet so the scene remains the focus.
5. Respect reduced-motion and static fallback paths without compromising the primary presentation.
